The method

Four steps, no distractions

  1. 1

    Set the mood

    Pick a preset or mix your own sounds. The window outside changes with them.

  2. 2

    Choose one task

    Write what you are working on and make it your focus. It sits above the timer.

  3. 3

    Focus

    Work until the chime. The timer stays accurate even when the tab is in the background.

  4. 4

    Rest, repeat

    Short breaks, a long break every few sessions, and a streak that grows quietly.

Not ready to start? Press ? for shortcuts, or just hit Space when you are.

        Timer settings

        Welcome to Hushroom

        A quiet place to study. No account, nothing to install, and it all stays on your device.

        1. Set the mood. Open the Sounds section, pick a preset like “Rainy library” and watch the window change with it.
        2. Pick one task. Add it in Tasks and press the target icon to make it your focus.
        3. Press Start. Work until the chime, rest, repeat. Your streak grows in Insights.
