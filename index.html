        Welcome to Hushroom

        A quiet place to study. No account, nothing to install, and it all stays on your device.

        1. Set the mood. Open the Mixer, pick a preset like “Rainy library” and watch the window change with it.
        2. Pick one task. Add it in Tasks and press the target icon to make it your focus.
        3. Press Start. Work until the chime, rest, repeat. Your streak grows in Stats.
